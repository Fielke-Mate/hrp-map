✈ Day 1 plan: hitch, with the bus as a floor. 12 Sep is a Saturday, and bus 965's weekend column runs only 08:45, 12:45, 16:10, 19:20 (€2 single) — the 08:58 arrival misses the 08:45 by 13 minutes. So: thumb it from the Lourdes station forecourt, and if nothing turns up, the 12:45 bus is the guaranteed fallback. Ask drivers for Pont d'Espagne rather than Cauterets — a direct lift skips the navette timetable altogether.
Navette on 12 Sep: 10:00 · 12:00 · 14:00 · 16:00 (the 31/08–26/09/26 reduced-summer table; journey ~20 min, €5.50 single). Which one you catch decides the day:
  10:00 → walking 10:30 → Cabane de Lourdes by 17:17.
  12:00 → walking 12:30 → Cabane de Lourdes by 19:17.
  14:00 (what the 12:45 bus feeds) → walking 14:30 → stop at Refuge de Bayssellance (km 11, staffed, 46pl) by 19:14. Day 2 then becomes 26.5 km.
  16:00 is too late to start walking at all.
Two numbers to remember: a lift before ~11:20 catches the 12:00 navette; after that a lift gains nothing over the bus, since both feed the 14:00. And you must be walking by 13:30 to reach Cabane de Lourdes before sunset (20:17, dark 20:45).
Bayssellance is the last shelter before the Hourquette d'Ossoue (2,734 m) — the risk in a late start is descending that col in the dark, not the finish time. Decide at Bayssellance, not before.
If the navette fails (the D920 closes at times for snow or maintenance): you can walk in via the Chemin des Cascades, about 1½ h from La Railère, summer only.
⚠ The 13:48 → 14:00 transfer is the weak point. Both are at the same place (Cauterets gare routière), but it is 12 minutes and the navette timetable warns times vary with weather and traffic. Miss it and the next navette is 16:00 — the last one in this band — reaching Pont d'Espagne at 16:20, too late to reach even Bayssellance before dark. (The 8h/18h departures belong to the 04/07–30/08 high-summer table, which has already ended by 12 Sep.) If that happens, do not wait for the 16:00: take a taxi for the last 8 km (~€20) or walk in via the Chemin des Cascades (~1½ h from La Railère).
